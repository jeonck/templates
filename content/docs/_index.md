## What this is

A library of the documents IT people are asked to produce and rarely get taught to write: charters, requirement specs, decision records, runbooks, incident reports, access reviews. Every page follows the same shape — when to reach for the document, a blank template you can copy, a filled-in example from a plausible project, and the failure modes that make this particular document useless.

Templates are deliberately short. A one-page artefact that gets maintained beats a fifteen-page one that is written once and abandoned at the first sprint boundary.

## How to use a template

Copy the fenced block, delete every section you cannot fill in honestly, and keep the document in version control next to the thing it describes. A template is a checklist of questions, not a form to complete — an empty heading left in place is worse than a heading removed, because it reads as "we considered this" when nobody did.

Each page ends with links to related templates, and every page shows which other pages link *to* it, so you can walk the library in either direction.
